VProg is a community for BME students interested in algorithms and competitive programming. At our club meetings, we solve problems in a relaxed and friendly atmosphere, learn from one another, and prepare for programming contests such as the ICPC.

Come along to our next meeting, or drop by our Discord server and say hello!

Language

We use English-language materials during our meetings. Whenever an international student is present, we ask everyone to speak English with one another, even if both people in the conversation also speak Hungarian.

This may feel a little unusual at first, but it quickly becomes natural. One of the main goals of our meetings is to build a community that also includes international students. It is also excellent preparation for a future international career in industry or academia.

Is VProg for you?

We recommend to join when you are already familiar with most of the material covered in the introductory SZIT courses (e.g. for software engineers ThAlg, ITC2).

If you are a first-year student, we recommend joining if you have already participated in a similar competition or have experience competing on platforms such as Codeforces.

What can you expect?

At our club meetings, in a relaxed and friendly atmosphere, we:

  • solve challenging problems from platforms such as Codeforces, AtCoder, and CSES;
  • develop our creative problem-solving and logical thinking skills;
  • learn algorithmic ideas, advanced algorithms, and data structures from one another;
  • practise writing clear and efficient code, as well as debugging;
  • discuss interesting topics in computer science and mathematics.
